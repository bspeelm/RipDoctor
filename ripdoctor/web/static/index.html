Punch a track

One track came out dirty — a skip, a click, a passage the stylus fought. Re-record just that track and swap it into the library. The boundaries are fitted against the archived side, so the replacement starts and ends exactly where the original did.

Drop the needle a few seconds before the track and lift it after — the fit needs music on both sides of it. Recording uses the capture controls on the right, and Stop & encode ends it like any other capture.

Replacing moves the old file and the punch capture into archive/<album>/_punched/. Nothing is deleted.

The 1–3 kHz bar is the one to watch. Arm-up handling rumble is loud full-band but dead in this band; silent groove is quiet in both; music is loud in both. Drop the needle when the band bar sits in the groove zone, not below it.

F / 0zoom to the whole side
Zzoom to the selected track
escstop playback
clickset the playhead — and seek there if audio is playing
dragon a marker moves it; on the background pans
wheelzoom about the cursor
stripthe bar under the waveform is the whole side — click or drag to jump

First pass

Looks up the release, splits the tracklist across every side by duration, and fits boundaries to the measured gaps. This replaces the plan for the whole album, not just the side you are viewing.

Archive rip

      
Puts the raw sides in archive/ — re-encoding any
        that are truncated, since a capture ended with Ctrl-C is not a valid FLAC —
        verifies every one, and only then removes raw/, the review
        folder and the analysis cache. Nothing in archive/ is ever
        deleted: the raw sides are what make a re-split possible without
        playing the record again.
